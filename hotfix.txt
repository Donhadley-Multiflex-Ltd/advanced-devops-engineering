# Hotfix

## Description

Following the successful resolution of the OAuth authentication issue,
login services have been fully restored for PlayStation users across all
affected regions.

As part of the hotfix validation process, the development team has extended
testing to Xbox users and other supported console platforms to ensure the
fix works consistently across the entire gaming ecosystem. Although the
issue was initially reported by PlayStation users, cross-platform testing
helps identify any hidden authentication dependencies and reduces the risk
of similar incidents occurring in the future.

By validating the hotfix across multiple console providers, the team can
confirm that OAuth authentication, Single Sign-On (SSO), and live streaming
integrations continue to function as expected for all users.

This proactive approach improves platform reliability, strengthens
authentication security, and helps prevent future authentication-related
outages.
